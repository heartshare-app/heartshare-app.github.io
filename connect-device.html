Device guide

How to connect your heart rate device

HeartShare reads any device that broadcasts heart rate over Bluetooth Low Energy. In most cases you only need to switch broadcasting on — don't pair the device in your phone's Bluetooth settings, the app finds it by itself.

Covers Apple Watch · Garmin watches · Garmin, Coospo, Moofit & other straps · Whoop · Xiaomi Mi Band 10

Devices

Before you start: wear the device so the sensor sits snug against your skin, make sure Bluetooth is on for your phone, and keep the device within a few metres. HeartShare scans automatically when you open the connection screen.

Apple Watch

Use the HeartShare watch app — it streams your pulse straight to your iPhone session.

Garmin watch

Garmin watches broadcast heart rate over ANT+/BLE once you enable it in the watch menu.

Menu → Health & Wellness → Heart Rate → Broadcast Heart Rate. Leave the broadcast screen open while you train.

Garmin HR strap & others

Coospo, Moofit, Polar, Wahoo and most other BLE chest straps and armbands work the same way.

Whoop

Turn on broadcasting in the Whoop app, then return to HeartShare.

Xiaomi Mi Band 10

Mi Band calls the feature "Share HR".

Device not showing up?

  • Unpair it from your phone's Bluetooth settings. A device already connected to the OS can't broadcast to the app.
  • Check broadcasting is still on. Most watches stop broadcasting when the screen or the broadcast view is closed.
  • Close other apps using the sensor — training apps hold an exclusive connection to the strap.
  • Restart the device and moisten the strap contacts; dry electrodes give no signal.

Do I need to pair my chest strap in Bluetooth settings?

No. Do not pair the strap in your phone's Bluetooth settings — HeartShare discovers broadcasting devices automatically. A device already connected to the operating system cannot broadcast to the app.
