What is HeartShare?

HeartShare is a real-time heart rate sharing app for athletes and coaches. The athlete broadcasts live BPM from a Bluetooth heart rate monitor or smartwatch, shares a short access key, and the coach follows the session live from their own phone — over Wi-Fi or mobile data, anywhere in the world.

How it works

Three steps from pairing to live coaching

No accounts to hand out, no cables, no waiting for the session to end to see the data.

1

Pair your monitor

Open HeartShare and connect a Bluetooth LE chest strap, armband or your smartwatch. Your live BPM appears instantly on screen.

2

Share your access key

Start a session and the app generates a short access key. Send it to your coach however you like — message, call, or read it out.

3

Train while they watch

Your coach enters the key and sees your pulse, zone and trend in real time, then reviews avg, max and min when the session ends.

FAQ

Questions about HeartShare, answered

Is HeartShare free?

Yes — HeartShare is free to download on iOS and Android, and the coach view is free to use with no subscription. A paid subscription removes ads and unlocks extended athlete features in the app.

Do coaches need to pay or create an account?

No. A coach only needs the athlete's access key. They enter it in the app and start watching the live session — no subscription is required for coach view.

How does a coach join my session?

Start sharing in the app, then send the generated access key to your coach. They open HeartShare, choose the coach role, enter the key and your live heart rate appears on their screen.

Which devices can HeartShare read heart rate from?

Any standard Bluetooth Low Energy heart rate monitor — chest straps, armbands and compatible smartwatches — plus Apple Watch on iOS. The pairing guide lists step-by-step instructions per device.

Does HeartShare work remotely?

Yes. Sharing runs over Wi-Fi or mobile data, so your coach can follow the session from another gym, another city or another country.

Is HeartShare available on Android?

Yes. HeartShare is on Google Play for Android phones, alongside the iOS version on the App Store. Athletes and coaches can be on different platforms in the same session.

What data does HeartShare collect?

Minimal data: your email or sign-in identifier, and subscription status so purchases can be restored across devices. The free version shows ads via AdMob, which may collect device identifiers.

We do not sell personal data to third parties. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Is HeartShare a medical device?

No. HeartShare is a training and fitness tool for sharing heart rate data between athletes and coaches. It is not intended for diagnosis or medical monitoring.
